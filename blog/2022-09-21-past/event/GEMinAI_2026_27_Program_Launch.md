The GEMinAI 2026-27 program launched at Concordia on Oct. 27th. Now in its 4th year, the program supports women and gender diverse students from all disciplines who will engage in AI through mentorship. New student mentees had the opportunity to meet their professional mentors for the first time and network with a growing community of AI professionals and practitioners.
