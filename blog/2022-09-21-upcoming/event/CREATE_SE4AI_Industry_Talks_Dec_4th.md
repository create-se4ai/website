**Title:**<br/>
Guardrails Aren't Enough: The Architectural Foundation for Regulated Health Data

**Abstract:**<br/>
When teams bring large language models into healthcare, the usual answer to safety is guardrails: prompt instructions, output filters and policy layers placed around the agent. Guardrails are useful, but on their own they can't guarantee that patient records stay where they belong. In this talk, Josh argues that this guarantee has to come from the architecture itself, and he uses Medeloop Analytics, an AI research platform that operates over regulated health data, as a case study.

The foundational decision is that the agent never sees raw records and works only with derived results. The analysis runs inside an isolated execution environment, which is the only place that holds the data and the credentials to reach it. Anything shown to a user passes through safety redaction tied to automated inspection. Generated documents leave through a separate export path with its own independent validation.

On top of that foundation, the platform:
- enforces authorization continuously, including on streaming connections
- records every decision, including refusals
- fails closed whenever access rights are uncertain
- extends the same boundary into the network, the model provider layer, environment separation and data residency

Medeloop traces each of these choices back to a principle that risk and compliance leadership set before any code was written, and also provides a practical checklist for evaluating any AI system proposed for regulated health data.

**About the Speaker:**

Josh Walonoski, Ph.D. is a life sciences and healthtech leader who works where science, regulation and technology meet. He is Co-Founder and Chief Risk and Compliance Officer of Medeloop, a company with operations in San Francisco, California and Montréal that builds AI agents for research on real-world health data. In that role he oversees AI governance, cybersecurity, legal and regulatory affairs. Since 2008, his career has spanned scientific consulting, regulatory strategy and commercial advisory work for the pharmaceutical, biotechnology and medical device industries, with roles at Innovative Science Solutions, Navigant Consulting and Boreal Strategy Advisors.
